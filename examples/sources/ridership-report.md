# Tidewater Transit: 2025 ridership report

Tidewater Transit carried 4.2 million trips in 2025, an increase of 11 percent over 2024 and the highest annual total since the agency was formed. Growth was strongest on weekdays, where average daily boardings rose from 12,900 to 14,300. Weekend ridership grew more slowly, at about 4 percent, and remains below its 2019 level.

The Harbor Ferry line, launched in March 2025, accounted for roughly a third of the year's growth. It carried 310,000 passengers in its first ten months, well above the forecast of 220,000. Two additional sailings were added in September to relieve crowding on the morning crossings.

On-time performance across the bus network was 91 percent, up from 88 percent the year before. The agency attributes the improvement to signal priority on the Route 7 corridor and to a new dispatch system introduced in May. Route 12 remained the least reliable line, at 79 percent on time, largely because of bridge construction.

Operating costs rose 6 percent to 48.5 million dollars, driven mainly by fuel and by overtime during the driver shortage in the first quarter. Fare revenue covered 31 percent of operating costs, compared with 29 percent in 2024.
