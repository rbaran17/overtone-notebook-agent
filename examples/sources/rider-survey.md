# Tidewater Transit: rider survey, autumn 2025

The autumn 2025 rider survey collected 1,840 responses between 6 October and 2 November, on board vehicles and online. Overall, 62 percent of respondents said they were satisfied or very satisfied with the service, up from 55 percent in the 2023 survey. Satisfaction was higher among riders who travel at least four days a week.

The most common complaint was evening frequency: 47 percent of respondents said buses after 8 p.m. do not run often enough, and many described waits of forty minutes or more. Cleanliness was the second most common complaint, raised by 22 percent, followed by real-time arrival information that did not match actual arrivals, at 18 percent.

Ferry riders were the most satisfied group, with 81 percent satisfied or very satisfied. Riders on Route 12 were the least satisfied, at 38 percent, and most of them cited unreliable arrival times as the reason.

Asked what would make them ride more often, respondents ranked more evening service first, lower fares second and safer stops third. Only 9 percent listed faster journeys, which suggests that reliability and frequency matter more to current riders than speed.
