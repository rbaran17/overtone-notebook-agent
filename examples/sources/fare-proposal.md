# Council memo: 2026 fare proposal

Staff recommend raising the base adult fare from 2.25 dollars to 2.50 dollars, effective 1 July 2026. It would be the first fare increase since 2019. The monthly pass would rise from 72 dollars to 78 dollars, while the reduced fare for seniors, students and low-income riders would stay at 1.10 dollars.

The increase is expected to raise about 1.9 million dollars a year in additional revenue, after allowing for an estimated 3 percent loss in ridership. Staff propose spending 1.2 million dollars of that on evening service: buses every 20 minutes until 11 p.m. on the six busiest routes.

The Tidewater Riders Union opposes the increase, arguing that it falls hardest on riders without alternatives and that the agency should first seek regional funding. The Chamber of Commerce supports it on the condition that the evening service commitment is written into the budget.

The council will hold a public hearing on 14 April 2026 and is scheduled to vote on 12 May 2026. If the proposal is rejected, staff say the planned evening service expansion would be deferred to 2027.
